## Full description
Limit: 4000 · Actual: **2867** characters

```text
Chùa An Nhiên ("the Temple of Serenity") is a fictional Vietnamese temple that fits in your pocket – a quiet corner where you can pause for a few minutes in a busy day. Light a stick of incense, listen to the bell, read a gentle message, then carry on with a lighter heart.

LIGHT VIRTUAL INCENSE
Tap to light an incense stick and watch the smoke drift slowly past the temple roof. No real smoke, no ash, no fire risk – fine for the office, a dorm room, or when you are far from home.

WOODEN FISH AND TEMPLE BELL
Tap the wooden fish (mõ) in a steady rhythm or ring the great bell and let it hum. Simple, natural sounds that help you slow down, breathe deeper and gather your thoughts.

DRAW A POEM FORTUNE – 100 ORIGINAL VERSES
Shake the fortune cup and receive one of 100 poems written especially for Chùa An Nhiên in the Vietnamese "lục bát" form, with an English rendering. Each poem comes with a general reading, readings by topic (family, work, health, study, love, finances) and a small piece of advice. There are no "bad" fortunes meant to frighten you: every poem points toward a positive path. The poems are for reflection, to help you look within; they are not predictions.

THE WISHING TREE
Write a wish for yourself, your family or all living beings and hang it on the Wishing Tree. Read anonymous wishes from others and feel a little less alone. Wishes are automatically filtered; if you see anything inappropriate you can report it or hide it instantly. Every report is reviewed.

A PEACEFUL MESSAGE EVERY DAY
One short, kind message each day – about gratitude, patience, family, and treating yourself and others gently.

LUNAR CALENDAR OF FESTIVALS
Follow the first and fifteenth days of each lunar month and the festivals of the year: Lunar New Year (Tết), the First Full Moon, Vesak, Vu Lan (Ullambana)… Each season the temple yard is decorated differently – apricot blossoms, lanterns, lotus flowers, roses – with gentle seasonal tasks, such as writing a thank-you note to your parents or a New Year wish for someone you love.

OUR PROMISES
• Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
• No account needed – just choose a nickname.
• We never ask for donations or offerings of any kind.
• Delete your data anytime from Settings.
• All poems, readings, messages and sounds are original works by our team.

GOOD TO KNOW
Chùa An Nhiên is a fictional temple and does not represent any real temple, religious organization or community. The app is made with respect for the Vietnamese tradition of visiting the temple, as a space for calm and reflection – it does not replace visiting a real temple, spiritual practice, or professional advice (medical, legal, financial). We do not promise that wishes will come true; we simply hope you find a few more peaceful minutes each day.

Feedback and support: hotro@chuaannhien.example
Wishing you a serene day.
```
