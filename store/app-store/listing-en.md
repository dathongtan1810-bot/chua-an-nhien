## Description
Limit: 4000 · Actual: **2320** characters

```text
Chùa An Nhiên ("the Temple of Serenity") is a fictional Vietnamese temple that fits in your pocket – a quiet corner to pause for a few minutes in a busy day. Light incense, listen to the bell, read a gentle message, then carry on with a lighter heart.

LIGHT VIRTUAL INCENSE
Tap to light an incense stick and watch the smoke drift past the temple roof. No real smoke, no ash – fine for the office, a dorm room, or when you are far from home.

WOODEN FISH AND TEMPLE BELL
Tap the wooden fish in a steady rhythm or ring the great bell and let it hum. Simple sounds that help you slow down and breathe deeper.

DRAW A POEM FORTUNE – 100 ORIGINAL VERSES
Shake the fortune cup and receive one of 100 poems written for Chùa An Nhiên in the Vietnamese "lục bát" form, with an English rendering, a general reading, readings by topic (family, work, health, study, love, finances) and a small piece of advice. No "bad" fortunes meant to frighten you. The poems are for reflection, to help you look within; they are not predictions.

THE WISHING TREE
Write a wish for yourself, your family or all living beings and hang it on the Wishing Tree; read anonymous wishes from others. Wishes are automatically filtered. You can report any wish, or block an author so you no longer see their wishes. Every report is reviewed.

A PEACEFUL MESSAGE EVERY DAY
One short, kind message each day about gratitude, patience, family and being gentle with yourself.

LUNAR CALENDAR OF FESTIVALS
New moon and full moon days, Lunar New Year (Tết), the First Full Moon, Vesak, Vu Lan… Each season the temple yard is decorated differently, with gentle tasks like writing a thank-you note to your parents or a New Year wish for a loved one.

OUR PROMISES
• Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
• No account needed – just a nickname.
• We never ask for donations or offerings of any kind.
• Delete your data anytime from Settings.
• All poems, readings, messages and sounds are original.

Chùa An Nhiên is fictional and does not represent any real temple or religious organization. It is a space for calm and reflection, not a replacement for visiting a temple, spiritual practice or professional advice. We do not promise that wishes will come true – we simply hope you find a few more peaceful minutes each day.

Support: hotro@chuaannhien.example
```
