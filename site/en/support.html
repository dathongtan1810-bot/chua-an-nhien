Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app creates an anonymous device code the first time you open it. A nickname is optional: if you don’t set one, your wishes appear on the Wishing Tree as “Someone”.

Where can I find my device code?

Open the app and go to Settings. Your device code is shown at the bottom, with a copy button. Include it when you contact us so we can help faster.

If I change phones, do my wishes come with me?

In version 1.0, data is tied to the device code, so it does not move to a new phone automatically. Fortune history stored on the old phone is not synced either.

Why doesn’t my wish appear on the Wishing Tree?

It may still be going through automatic filtering, or it may have been hidden because it contained inappropriate words, links, phone numbers or personal information. Try writing it again more briefly and gently. If you think the filter made a mistake, contact us.

Are the fortunes accurate?

Fortunes are for reflection, to help you look into your own heart; they are not predictions. Please don’t use them in place of professional advice on health, money or legal matters.

A festival date in the app differs from my local calendar.

Lunar calendars can differ slightly between sources; in short months, day 30 moves back to the last day of the month. If you spot an error, please tell us the exact date.

Does the app charge money or accept offerings?

No. Version 1.0 is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no offerings. If anyone asks you for money in the name of Chùa An Nhiên, it is not us – please let us know.

Reporting an inappropriate wish

If you see a wish that is obscene, insulting, hateful, sexual or violent, a scam or advertisement, contains someone else’s personal information, or insults a religion:

  1. On the Wishing Tree, press and hold the wish (or tap the “⋯” icon).
  2. Choose Report and select the most fitting reason.
  3. The wish is hidden from your screen immediately; our team reviews it and removes it if it breaks the rules, usually within 24 hours.

If you no longer want to see someone’s wishes, press and hold one of their wishes and choose Block author: all wishes from that author are hidden on your device. You can unblock in Settings.

You can also email hotro@chuaannhien.example with the wish text, when you saw it, and a screenshot if possible. Devices with repeated violations may be banned under our Terms of Use.

Deleting your data

This page applies to the Chùa An Nhiên app published by the Chùa An Nhiên development team. You can request deletion of your data without uninstalling the app.

Option 1 – In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the Chùa An Nhiên app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my data.
  4. Read the notice and tap Confirm deletion.

Your data is deleted from our servers immediately, local data on the device is cleared, and the app generates a new device code.

Option 2 – By email (if you cannot open the app)

  1. Email hotro@chuaannhien.example with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include your device code (found in Settings). If you no longer have it, describe your nickname, the content or the time you posted wishes so we can locate the data.
  3. We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 30 days at most.

Data that is deleted

  • Your anonymous device code and its link to your data.
  • Your nickname.
  • All wishes you have hung (including those currently shown on the Wishing Tree).
  • Reports you have sent.
  • Data stored on the device (fortune history, settings) when you delete in the app.

Data kept temporarily

  • Encrypted backups: up to 30 days, then fully overwritten.
  • Technical crash logs (no wish content): up to 90 days.
  • Violation records (wishes removed for breaking the rules and related reports): up to 12 months, only to handle appeals and prevent repeat abuse.
  • Any data we are legally required to keep, for the period required by law.

Uninstalling the app does not delete data on our servers. See also our Privacy Policy.
