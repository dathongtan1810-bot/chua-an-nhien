Small rituals, every day

No need to travel far. A few quiet minutes are enough to lighten the heart.

  • Virtual incense

    Choose a stick, tap to light it and sit for a moment with the thin rising smoke. No ash, no cost.

  • Poem fortunes

    One hundred original verses in Vietnamese lục bát form, with English versions and reflections on family, work, health, study, love and finances.

  • The Wishing Tree

    Write a short wish and hang it on the tree. Wishes appear anonymously and are filtered automatically to keep the tree kind.

  • Daily message of peace

    Every day of the year brings a short, gentle note to carry with you through the morning.

  • Lunar festival calendar

    New-moon and full-moon days and traditional festivals, with decoration ideas and small things to do on the day.

  • Vietnamese & English

    All verses, messages and calendar entries come in both languages, so you can share them with family far away.

A verse to reflect on

Each fortune is a four-line poem with a few gentle thoughts – it never declares good or bad luck.

Sample · Verse 53

The Lotus in the Pond

A lotus rises pink from muddy ground Its fragrance pure, its color clear and sound A gentle soul keeps one heart true and bright Untouched by dust, it walks the days in light

Sen hồng mọc giữa bùn ao Hương thơm vẫn tỏa, sắc nào kém trong Người hiền giữ một tấm lòng Bụi đường chẳng nhuốm, thong dong tháng ngày

The lotus grows out of mud yet keeps its fragrance and its color. You do not need to change the whole pond; you only need to keep your own heart clear. When you live kindly, others will gradually feel it, just as lotus fragrance travels far without anyone announcing it.

Advice: When something annoys you today, take three deep breaths before you answer, and keep your words gentle.

This verse is for reflection, to help you look within; it is not a prediction.

Our promises

A place for stillness must first be trustworthy.

  • No superstition

    Fortunes are poems for reflection, not predictions. We never promise miracles or label a verse as lucky or unlucky.

  • No ads

    No advertisements interrupting your quiet moments, and no advertising identifiers.

  • No paid offerings

    Incense, fortunes and wishes are all free. The app never asks for or accepts donations or offerings of any kind.

  • Respect for privacy

    No phone number or email needed. Just an anonymous device code and a nickname you choose – and you can delete your data at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is An Nhiên Temple a real temple?

No. Chùa An Nhiên is a fictional temple created inside the game as a space for quiet reflection. The app does not represent any real temple, organization or religious body.

Do the fortunes predict the future?

No. Each fortune is an original four-line poem with gentle suggestions for reflection. Please do not use them in place of advice from a doctor, lawyer or financial professional.

Does the app cost anything?

Version 1.0 is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. We also never accept offerings or donations.

Who can read my wishes?

Wishes hung on the Wishing Tree are visible to other players together with the nickname you chose (or “Someone” if you didn’t set one), but without identifying information. Please do not write anyone’s full name, phone number or address.

I found an inappropriate wish. What should I do?

Press and hold the wish and choose “Report”. Our team will review it and remove content that breaks the rules. See the Support page for more.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data. If you can no longer access the app, follow the data deletion instructions.
