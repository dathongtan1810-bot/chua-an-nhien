Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 · Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how the Chùa An Nhiên app (An Nhiên Temple; “the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects data when you use the app on Android and iOS, and the rights you have over your data. We collect as little as possible: you do not need to give us your name, phone number or email address to use the app.

In short: we only use an anonymous device code generated by the app, a nickname you choose, and the wishes you write. No ads, no advertising identifiers, and we never sell data. You can delete your data at any time in Settings → Delete my data.

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is the Chùa An Nhiên development team (Nhóm phát triển Chùa An Nhiên). Please send any data-related request to hotro@chuaannhien.example.

An Nhiên Temple is a fictional temple inside a game. The app does not belong to or represent any real temple, religious organization or religious body.

2. Data we collect

Data typeDetailsRequired?

Some data stays only on your device and is never sent to our servers, for example your fortune history, light/dark theme, language and festival reminder settings.

We do not collect: real names, phone numbers, email addresses (unless you email us), contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers or payment information. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. How we use data

  • To run the features: storing and showing wishes on the Wishing Tree and linking wishes to your device so you can view, edit or delete them.
  • To keep the community safe: automatically filtering inappropriate content, handling reports, and preventing abuse and spam.
  • To maintain and improve quality: detecting, diagnosing and fixing technical errors.
  • To answer support requests and requests to exercise your data rights.
  • To comply with legal obligations when there is a valid request from a competent authority.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing profiles, or automated decisions that have legal effects on you.

4. Legal bases

  • Your consent: when you set a nickname and write and publish a wish. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the content or all your data; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before.
  • Providing the service you request: the anonymous device code is needed for the app to work and to recognise your own content.
  • Legitimate interests / safety: content filtering, handling reports, preventing abuse and minimal crash logging.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires it.

We process personal data in line with Vietnamese personal data protection law, including Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP and other applicable regulations, as well as Google Play and Apple App Store policies. Where other data protection laws apply to you (such as the GDPR), we respect the rights they give you.

5. Sharing

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data with anyone.
  • We only use infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, database, crash reporting) acting as data processors on our instructions and bound by contractual confidentiality.
  • Public wishes: wish content and your nickname are shown to other players on the Wishing Tree. Your device code is never shown publicly.
  • When required by law: in response to a valid request from a competent authority, or to protect the safety of users.

Our infrastructure providers’ servers may be located outside Vietnam. In that case, transfers are made with appropriate safeguards and in compliance with the procedures required by Vietnamese law.

6. Storage and retention

  • Device code, nickname, wishes: kept until you delete the wish or delete your data. If a device does not connect to the service for 24 consecutive months, we may delete the associated data.
  • Wishes removed for violations and report records: kept for up to 12 months to handle appeals and prevent repeat abuse, then deleted.
  • Technical crash logs: kept for up to 90 days.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being fully overwritten.

7. Security

  • All data travelling between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Server-side data is stored on infrastructure with encryption at rest and access controls; only team members who need access have it.
  • We follow data minimisation and review our safeguards regularly.

No system is perfectly secure. If an incident affects your data, we will notify you and respond as required by law.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Be informed and access the data we hold about you (your wishes appear under “My wishes” in the app; you can also request a copy by email).
  • Rectify: change your nickname, and edit or remove your wishes in the app.
  • Erase: delete individual wishes or all your data (see section 9).
  • Withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing as permitted by law.
  • Complain to us or to the competent data protection authority.

Because we do not know your real identity, please include the device code shown in the app’s Settings when you email us, so we can find the right data. We respond within 30 days at most.

9. How to delete your data

  1. In the app: open Settings → Delete my data and confirm. Data linked to your device code (nickname, wishes, reports you sent) is deleted from our servers immediately, and the app generates a new device code.
  2. By email: if you have uninstalled the app or cannot open it, email hotro@chuaannhien.example with the subject “Data deletion request”, including your device code if you have it. We process requests within 30 days at most.

Details on what is deleted and what is kept temporarily are on the Support – Delete my data page. Uninstalling the app only removes data on your device; it does not delete data on our servers.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Users under 16 should use the app with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian, in line with Vietnamese law on protecting children’s personal data and the equivalent rules where you live (such as COPPA in the United States).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact hotro@chuaannhien.example and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or when the law requires. The new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. For significant changes (for example, collecting a new type of data), we will notify you in the app and ask for your consent where required.
